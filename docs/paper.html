Live mass paper test

Virtual money only. No accounts, no real orders. Every 5 minutes the engine opens a simulated BUY and SELL on every instrument at every horizon (30s to 6h; 1h and longer every 30 minutes), whatever it expects, and settles from live public prices. This measures what works. A group is only called significant if it survives multiple-testing correction (Benjamini-Hochberg 5%) with a conservative effective sample size. Not financial advice.
